TalkBack German Translator is an accessibility companion app for Android and TalkBack.
It captures foreign on-screen text from third-party apps, games, or websites and translates
it directly into German, reading it aloud via the Android Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine.

Features:
- Tailored for TalkBack and screen reader users
- Accessibility service with gesture triggers
- Quick Settings tile in notification shade for quick toggling
- On-device translation support
- Auditory earcons and haptic vibration feedback
- Customizable speech rate and pitch
- Free and open source under MIT License
